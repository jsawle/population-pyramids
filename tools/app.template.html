About this map

A population pyramid shows how many males and females there are in each 5-year age group. Wide bases point to many young people; straighter sides and wide tops point to an older population.

How to use it

  • Move the pointer over a country to see its pyramid. Click a country to pin it so you can read it closely; click the sea, press Esc or choose Unpin to go back.
  • On touch screens, tap a country. You can also pick any country or area from the Country list.
  • Use the Year slider (or Play) to see how a pyramid changes from 1950 to 2100. Years up to 2020 are UN estimates; 2025 onwards are UN projections (medium variant).
  • Bars show each group's share of the whole population, so countries of different sizes can be compared. The scale is fixed for each country across all years, so you can watch it change. Use “Show as table” to read the exact values.
  • Countries on the map are shaded by median age: half the population is younger than this, half older.

Data and method

Population by age and sex comes from the United Nations World Population Prospects 2024 (via the wpp2024 data package), licensed CC BY 3.0 IGO. The UN has not reviewed or endorsed this map. Figures are for 1 July of each year, grouped into 5-year age bands (100+ as the top band). Totals were checked against the UN's published figures (for example, world population in 2025: 8,231,613,070). Median age was checked against the UN's published values.

Country boundaries: Esri World Countries (Generalized), sources: Esri; Garmin International, Inc.; U.S. Central Intelligence Agency (The World Factbook); National Geographic Society. Some territories (for example Antarctica) have no separate UN figures; a few small areas are counted within another country by the UN.
